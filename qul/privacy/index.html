This privacy policy explains how Iqra AI Labs (Dhaka, Bangladesh) ("we", "us") handles information in the Qul AI Android app (Google Play title: "Qul AI: Quran & Qaida"). We collect only what the app needs to work. We do not require an account, we do not show ads, and we do not sell your data.

01Information we collect

Voice recordings (microphone)

When you use pronunciation practice, the app records your voice. The recording is sent over HTTPS to our server so our AI can score your pronunciation. It is processed temporarily and is not stored after the result is returned to you.

Approximate location (optional)

If you allow it, the app uses your approximate location only to calculate prayer times. You can choose your city manually instead. Your location is not stored on our server.

Crash and diagnostic data

The app sends anonymous crash logs and performance data so we can find and fix bugs.

What we do not collect

  • No account or login is needed.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or payment information.
  • The app contains no ads.

02How we use it

  • Voice recordings: only to score your pronunciation and show you the result.
  • Approximate location: only to calculate prayer times for your area.
  • Crash and diagnostic data: only to fix bugs and improve the app's stability and performance.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

03Permissions

MicrophoneRequired for practice

Needed to record your voice for pronunciation practice.

LocationOptional

Approximate location for prayer times. You can deny it and pick your city manually.

You can change these permissions at any time in your device's Android settings.

04Data sharing

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. We share data only with service providers that help us run the app:

  • Hosting: the servers that process voice recordings for scoring.
  • Crash reporting: the service that receives anonymous crash and performance data.

These providers may use the data only to provide their services to us.

05Security

All data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

06Retention & deletion

  • Voice recordings are not stored after your pronunciation result is returned.
  • Location is not stored on our server.
  • Crash and diagnostic data is anonymous and kept only as long as needed to fix bugs.

You can ask us to delete any data related to you by emailing masumcsepust@gmail.com.

07Children's privacy

Qul AI is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

08Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and change the "Last updated" date at the top.
