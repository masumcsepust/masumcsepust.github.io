Privacy Policy

App: Qul AI  •  Developer: Iqra AI Labs  •  Last updated: 18 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Iqra AI Labs ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when you use the Qul AI app ("the App"), an app for learning Arabic letter pronunciation (Qaida) and Quran recitation.

1. Information we collect

  • Voice recordings (microphone): When you use pronunciation practice, the App records your voice. The recording is sent securely to our server only to analyse and score your pronunciation. Recordings are processed temporarily and are not stored after the result is returned, and are not used to identify you.
  • Location (optional): If you allow it, the App uses your approximate location only to calculate prayer times. You can instead choose your city manually. Location is not stored on our servers.
  • Crash and diagnostic data: We may collect anonymous crash logs and app performance data to fix bugs and improve stability.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or payment information. We do not require an account.

2. How we use information

  • To provide pronunciation feedback and scoring
  • To show accurate prayer times
  • To fix crashes and improve the App

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Permissions

  • Microphone: required for pronunciation practice only.
  • Location: optional, for prayer times only.

You can revoke permissions at any time in your device settings.

4. Data sharing

We do not share personal data with third parties, except service providers that help us run the App (such as cloud hosting and crash reporting), who process data only on our behalf.

5. Security

All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

6. Data retention and deletion

Voice recordings are deleted immediately after processing. Crash data is kept only as long as needed to fix issues. To request deletion of any data associated with your device, contact us at the email below.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
